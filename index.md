These notes develop a nonlinear hydropower model from physical equations and connect it to frequency-control theory, acausal modeling, digital control, AGC, and numerical methods.

## Roadmap

The long-term goal is to move from the nonlinear physical model

$$
F(\dot{x},x,z,u)=0
$$

to equilibrium, linearization, state-space models, transfer functions, and finally FCR/LFC analysis.
