Governor and Droop Control

This note develops governor control analytically, beginning with isochronous control and then introducing droop for primary frequency response.

1. Frequency error

Let synchronous speed be $\omega_s$ and define

$$\Delta\omega=\omega-\omega_s.$$

The control error can be written

$$e_\omega=\omega_{\rm ref}-\omega.$$

2. Isochronous control

In ideal isochronous operation, the objective is zero steady-state speed error. A PI governor is

$$\dot\xi=e_\omega,$$

and

$$u_g=K_pe_\omega+K_i\xi.$$

If the actuator is first order,

$$T_g\dot y_g=-y_g+u_g.$$

The complete governor becomes

$$\boxed{T_g\dot y_g=-y_g+K_p(\omega_{\rm ref}-\omega)+K_i\xi}$$

with

$$\boxed{\dot\xi=\omega_{\rm ref}-\omega}.$$

3. Why integral action restores frequency

At equilibrium, $\dot\xi=0$, hence

$$\omega^\star=\omega_{\rm ref}.$$

This is the mathematical reason an ideal isochronous controller eliminates steady-state frequency error.

4. Droop control

For primary frequency control, generator power reference changes proportionally with frequency deviation:

$$\boxed{\Delta P_{\rm ref}=-\frac{1}{R}\Delta\omega}.$$

The parameter $R$ is the droop coefficient. A decrease in frequency, $\Delta\omega<0$, produces an increase in commanded power.

5. Governor with droop and actuator dynamics

A simple model is

$$T_g\dot y_g=-y_g+K_g\left(P_0-\frac{1}{R}\Delta\omega\right).$$

In perturbation variables,

$$T_g\Delta\dot y_g=-\Delta y_g-\frac{K_g}{R}\Delta\omega.$$

After Laplace transformation,

$$\Delta Y_g(s)=-\frac{K_g/R}{T_gs+1}\Delta\Omega(s).$$

Therefore the governor transfer function is

$$\boxed{G_g(s)=\frac{\Delta Y_g(s)}{\Delta\Omega(s)}=-\frac{K_g/R}{T_gs+1}}.$$

6. Coupling to the turbine

Suppose the linearized turbine-hydraulic dynamics are represented by

$$\Delta P_m(s)=G_h(s)\Delta Y_g(s).$$

Then

$$\Delta P_m(s)=-G_h(s)\frac{K_g/R}{T_gs+1}\Delta\Omega(s).$$

The primary-control path is therefore

$$\boxed{G_{P_m\omega}(s)=-G_h(s)\frac{K_g/R}{T_gs+1}}.$$

7. Coupling to the swing equation

The linearized generator-frequency equation is

$$M\Delta\dot\omega=\Delta P_m-\Delta P_e-D\Delta\omega.$$

For a simple aggregated load disturbance $\Delta P_L$, one often writes

$$M\Delta\dot\omega=\Delta P_m-\Delta P_L-D\Delta\omega.$$

In Laplace form,

$$\Delta\Omega(s)=\frac{\Delta P_m(s)-\Delta P_L(s)}{Ms+D}.$$

8. Closed-loop characteristic equation

Using droop feedback, the closed-loop denominator becomes

$$1+\frac{G_h(s)K_g/R}{(T_gs+1)(Ms+D)}=0,$$

or equivalently

$$\boxed{(T_gs+1)(Ms+D)+\frac{K_g}{R}G_h(s)=0}.$$

This equation determines small-signal stability.

9. Steady-state frequency deviation with droop

For a constant disturbance and a simplified static turbine gain $K_t$, the final-value relation gives

$$0=\Delta P_m-\Delta P_L-D\Delta\omega.$$

With

$$\Delta P_m=-\frac{K_tK_g}{R}\Delta\omega,$$

we obtain

$$\boxed{\Delta\omega_{\rm ss}=-\frac{\Delta P_L}{D+K_tK_g/R}}.$$

Droop reduces the frequency deviation but does not generally restore frequency exactly to nominal.

10. Load sharing between generators

For two generators with droops $R_1$ and $R_2$,

$$\Delta P_1=-\frac{1}{R_1}\Delta\omega,$$

and

$$\Delta P_2=-\frac{1}{R_2}\Delta\omega.$$

Hence

$$\frac{\Delta P_1}{\Delta P_2}=\frac{R_2}{R_1}.$$

Droop therefore provides decentralized proportional sharing of a system imbalance.

11. Saturation and practical nonlinearities

A real guide vane satisfies

$$y_{\min}\le y_g\le y_{\max},$$

and rate limits may be represented by

$$\dot y_{\min}\le\dot y_g\le\dot y_{\max}.$$

These nonlinearities can strongly affect large-disturbance FCR behavior even when the small-signal transfer function is stable.

12. Isochronous versus droop

Isochronous control seeks

$$\Delta\omega_{\rm ss}=0,$$

while droop intentionally permits

$$\Delta\omega_{\rm ss}\neq0$$

so that multiple units can share primary response without fighting each other.

13. Compact model

A useful primary-control model is $$\dot\xi=e_\omega,$$ $$T_g\dot y_g=-y_g+K_pe_\omega+K_i\xi-\frac{K_d}{R}\Delta\omega,$$ coupled to the nonlinear hydraulic DAE. The exact controller structure depends on whether the operating mode is isochronous, droop, or a combination with supervisory set-point control.
